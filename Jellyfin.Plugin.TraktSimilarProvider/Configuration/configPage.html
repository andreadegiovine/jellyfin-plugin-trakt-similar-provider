Trakt Similar Provider

The "Trakt" provider for the Similar carousel (movies and shows) and for the Suggestions tab ("Because you watched..." and "Because you liked...") uses the public Trakt.tv "related" data. No Trakt account is needed, and nothing has to be configured for the plugin to work.

Settings

How long Trakt responses are kept in memory. The same cache is shared by Similar items and Suggestions.
